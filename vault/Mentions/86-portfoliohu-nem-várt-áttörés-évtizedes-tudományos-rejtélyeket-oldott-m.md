## Summary

Hungarian business daily Portfolio reports on AI independently solving long-standing scientific problems and cites the recently published Leiden Declaration for its guidelines on ethical AI use in mathematics, in particular on authorship: a machine cannot bear scientific responsibility, but a substantive contribution must be disclosed.
