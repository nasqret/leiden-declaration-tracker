# Thoughts about the Leiden Declaration

> **Outlet:** [[Gowers's Weblog (Timothy Gowers)]] · **Type:** ✍️ Blogs · **Author:** Timothy Gowers · **Date:** 2026-07-26 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United Kingdom (country)\|United Kingdom]]

**Link:** https://gowers.wordpress.com/2026/07/26/thoughts-about-the-leiden-declaration/

## Summary

A long post by Fields Medallist Timothy Gowers, who attended the Lorentz Center workshop that led to the declaration, setting out why he did not sign it. He walks through the declaration's five stated values and eleven recommendations, agrees with much of the diagnosis, and registers uncertainty about parts he sees as tied to preserving the status quo — particularly the emphasis on human authorship and attribution, which he notes is already subjective and shaped by gatekeeping and politics. He concludes the declaration makes an important and positive contribution even if imperfect.

## Key quote

> If we arrive at a world where mathematical theorems are no longer associated with mathematicians, maybe that won't be problematic.
