# 莱顿宣言：在人工智能时代，数学的未来不能只剩下机器的答案

> **Outlet:** [[澎湃新闻 (The Paper)]] · **Type:** 📰 Newspapers & wire services · **Author:** 金威 (trans. 赵思怡) · **Date:** 2026-06-17 · **Language:** Chinese · **Country:** [[China (country)\|China]]

**Link:** https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33377868

## Summary

A long feature in Shanghai daily The Paper on the Leiden Declaration, reporting 2,411 signatories at the time of writing and setting out the declaration's concerns: erosion of peer review, weakened attribution because models are trained on published mathematics, incentive structures tilted toward 'AI-friendly' problems, and loss of research autonomy as technology companies shape priorities. It stresses the declaration's insistence that mathematical understanding requires human participation.

## Key quote

> 人类无法读懂的证明，对数学共同体而言等同于不存在。
