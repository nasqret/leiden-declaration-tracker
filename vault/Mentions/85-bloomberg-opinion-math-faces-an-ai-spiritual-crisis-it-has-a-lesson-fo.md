# Math Faces an AI 'Spiritual Crisis.' It Has a Lesson for the Rest of Us

> **Outlet:** [[Bloomberg Opinion]] · **Type:** 📰 Newspapers & wire services · **Author:** Parmy Olson · **Date:** 2026-08-13 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United States (country)\|United States]]

## Summary

A Bloomberg Opinion column arguing that AI's advances in mathematics — including the Jacobian conjecture counterexample — pose a crisis of meaning for the discipline, and that the lesson for other fields is to value judgment and the choice of questions over cheaply produced outputs. It describes the Leiden Declaration as an 11-page document cautioning that unchecked automation threatens not only how mathematics is practised but what the discipline stands for.
