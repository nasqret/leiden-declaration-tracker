# The crisis of AI-generated mathematics

> **Outlet:** [[arXiv (preprint, math.HO)]] · **Type:** 📄 Preprints · **Author:** Max Weinreich (Harvard University) · **Date:** 2026-08-03 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United States (country)\|United States]]

**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02859

## Summary

A math.HO preprint arguing for opposition to the use of AI in mathematics and proposing steps for individuals, departments, journals and institutions. It positions the Leiden Declaration between Terence Tao's optimism and Jacob Tsimerman's more absolute objections, describing it as an opt-in political statement that recommends standards for proof, authorship and human oversight while leaving signatories room for interpretation.

## Key quote

> The Leiden Declaration stands somewhere in between as an opt-in political statement recommending standards for structures of proof, authorship, and human oversight, while leaving signatories generous room for interpretation.
