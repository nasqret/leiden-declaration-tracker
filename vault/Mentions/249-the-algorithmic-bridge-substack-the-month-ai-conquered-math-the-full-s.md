## Summary

A long-form essay recounting the sequence of AI results in mathematics through mid-2026 and the discipline's reaction. It quotes the Leiden Declaration repeatedly — on mathematics producing 'understanding, clarity, and judgment' as well as results, on the autonomous shaping of research direction, and on AI outputs failing to cite the human work they synthesise — using it to frame arguments about verification bottlenecks and attribution.
