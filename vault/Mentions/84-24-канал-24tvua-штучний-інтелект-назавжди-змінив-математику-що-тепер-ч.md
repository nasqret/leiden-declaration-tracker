## Summary

A Ukrainian news report on how AI has changed mathematical discovery, citing the Leiden Declaration — signed by thousands of mathematicians worldwide — for the position that AI should complement rather than replace human mathematical creativity, and for its requirements of transparency, accountability and proper attribution.
