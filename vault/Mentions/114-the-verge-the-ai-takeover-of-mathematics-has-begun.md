# The AI takeover of mathematics has begun

> **Outlet:** [[The Verge]] · **Type:** 🧪 Science & tech magazines · **Author:** Robert Hart · **Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United States (country)\|United States]]

**Link:** https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/977273/the-ai-takeover-of-mathematics-has-begun

## Summary

A Verge feature on AI systems producing research-level mathematics, built on interviews with working mathematicians including Colva Roney-Dougal. It reports the Leiden Declaration as the collective response, noting it is endorsed by the International Mathematical Union and had been signed by more than 3,400 people, and that it urges policymakers, governments and media not to buy into companies' hype.

## Key quote

> In June, mathematicians published the Leiden Declaration, a set of principles for the responsible use of AI in mathematics that has been endorsed by the International Mathematical Union and signed by more than 3,400 people.
