## Summary

Smithsonian's Smart News covers the AI-assisted counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture and cites the Leiden Declaration as the mathematical community's formal response, summarising its calls to disclose AI use, credit earlier work, publish in peer-reviewed outlets, and avoid announcing claims before the community can evaluate them.

## Key quote

> After that 'Erdős problem' was solved with the help of A.I., 16 mathematicians published the Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics, which is endorsed by the International Mathematical Union.
