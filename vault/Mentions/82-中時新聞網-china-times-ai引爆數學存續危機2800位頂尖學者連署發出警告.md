# AI引爆數學存續危機！2800位頂尖學者連署發出警告

> **Outlet:** [[中時新聞網 (China Times)]] · **Type:** 📰 Newspapers & wire services · **Author:** 蔡文英 · **Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Language:** Chinese · **Country:** [[Taiwan (country)\|Taiwan]]

**Link:** https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20260706002293-260408

## Summary

Taiwanese daily China Times reports that more than 2,800 mathematicians, including Fields medallists, had signed the Leiden Declaration, and summarises its warnings about unrestrained use of AI in proofs, the difficulty of distinguishing AI output from rigorous proof, and results announced on unreviewed platforms. It draws on Washington Post and Asahi Shimbun reporting.

## Key quote

> 若不加節制地在數學證明中使用AI，可能帶來風險。
