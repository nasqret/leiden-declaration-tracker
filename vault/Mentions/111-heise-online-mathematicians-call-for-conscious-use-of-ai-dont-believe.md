# Mathematicians call for conscious use of AI: 'Don't believe the hype'

> **Outlet:** [[heise online]] · **Type:** 🧪 Science & tech magazines · **Author:** Daniel AJ Sokolov · **Date:** 2026-07-27 · **Language:** German · **Country:** [[Germany (country)\|Germany]]

**Link:** https://www.heise.de/en/news/Mathematicians-call-for-conscious-use-of-AI-Don-t-believe-the-hype-11378215.html

## Summary

heise online's report on the presentation of the Leiden Declaration at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Philadelphia. It summarises the declaration's warnings — AI output that is hard to distinguish from valid proof, inadequate attribution, and corporations steering research toward AI-tractable problems — and its recommendations grouped for individual researchers, organisations and funders, and policymakers.

## Key quote

> Mathematicians should therefore withdraw from harmful work. As a preventive measure, they should only enter into partnerships with outsiders if these support the values expressed by the Leiden Declaration.
