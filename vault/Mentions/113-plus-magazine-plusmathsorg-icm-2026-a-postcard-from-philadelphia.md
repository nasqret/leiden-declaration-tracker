# ICM 2026: A postcard from Philadelphia

> **Outlet:** [[Plus Magazine (plus.maths.org)]] · **Type:** 🧪 Science & tech magazines · **Author:** Marianne Freiberger and Rachel Thomas · **Date:** 2026-08-05 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United Kingdom (country)\|United Kingdom]]

**Link:** https://plus.maths.org/icm-2026-postcard-philadelphia

## Summary

A report from the International Congress of Mathematicians (Philadelphia, 23–30 July 2026) by the editors of Plus Magazine. In the section on publishing it records that a panel organised by the IMU Committee on Publishing featured extensive discussion of the Leiden Declaration and of whether decisions about publishing in the age of AI should be made bottom-up or top-down; the report notes attendees generally favoured decentralised decision-making.
