# AIが数学の証明まで書く時代へ。ライデン宣言が問う「人間の役割」

> **Outlet:** [[天秤AIメディア byGMO]] · **Type:** ✍️ Blogs · **Author:** 星川アイナ · **Date:** 2026-06-17 · **Language:** Japanese · **Country:** [[Japan (country)\|Japan]]

**Link:** https://tenbin.ai/media/ai_insights/leiden-declaration-ai-math

## Summary

A Japanese explainer from GMO's Tenbin AI media on the declaration: written by 16 researchers from 15 universities and backed by the IMU, it sets shared principles for responsible AI use rather than excluding AI, and identifies five concerns — plausible but incorrect proofs, inadequate citation of human sources, widening disparities between researchers, overstated claims, and loss of mathematical independence.

## Key quote

> Mathematics is, and should always remain, a profoundly human endeavour
