# Mathematicians are grappling with the possibility that AI might eclipse them

> **Outlet:** [[Understanding AI (Substack)]] · **Type:** 📨 Newsletters · **Author:** Kai Williams · **Date:** 2026-08-04 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United States (country)\|United States]]

**Link:** https://www.understandingai.org/p/mathematicians-are-grappling-with

## Summary

A long newsletter piece surveying how mathematicians are responding to AI systems producing research-level results. It treats the Leiden Declaration as the most prominent articulated response, summarising its list of 'characteristic values of mathematical research' and its recommendations, while describing it as a starting point rather than a complete vision of mathematics' future.

## Key quote

> The most prominent attempt to articulate a human response to AI's impact on mathematics has been the Leiden Declaration, which arose from a September 2025 conference.
