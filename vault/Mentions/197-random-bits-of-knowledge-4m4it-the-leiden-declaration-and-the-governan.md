# The Leiden Declaration and the Governance of AI-Assisted Mathematics

> **Outlet:** [[Random Bits of Knowledge (4m4.it)]] · **Type:** ✍️ Blogs · **Author:** Antonio Montano · **Date:** 2026-06-04 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[Italy (country)\|Italy]]

**Link:** https://4m4.it/posts/leiden-declaration-and-the-governance-of-ai-assisted-mathematics/

## Summary

A blog essay reading the declaration as a governance framework rather than a rejection of AI: proof as understanding, human authorship as accountability, attribution as intellectual memory, and public infrastructure as epistemic sovereignty. It distinguishes 'proof pipelines' from 'meaning pipelines' and argues formal verification is necessary but not sufficient, with dependency on proprietary AI infrastructure as the central risk.

## Key quote

> A proof object answers one question: Does this conclusion follow from these premises by valid inference? Mathematical practice answers a larger set of questions: What is the right statement? Why is it true?
