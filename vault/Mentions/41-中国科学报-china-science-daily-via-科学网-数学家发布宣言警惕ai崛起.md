# 数学家发布宣言，警惕AI崛起

> **Outlet:** [[中国科学报 (China Science Daily, via 科学网)]] · **Type:** 📰 Newspapers & wire services · **Author:** 李木子 · **Date:** 2026-06-04 · **Language:** Chinese · **Country:** [[China (country)\|China]]

**Link:** https://news.sciencenet.cn/sbhtmlnews/2026/6/389926.shtm

## Summary

China Science Daily reports the publication of the 11-page Leiden Declaration by 16 mathematicians on 2 June, describing its warning that uncontrolled automation threatens both mathematical practice and the discipline's humanistic values, and its argument that mathematics is more than a tool for producing correct answers.

## Key quote

> 数学不仅仅是一种用于得出正确答案的工具，更是一项极具人文色彩的事业。
