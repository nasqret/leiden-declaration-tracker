# The Dark Night of Mathematics

> **Outlet:** [[Ossuary Lost at Sea (Kirwin Hampshire, Substack)]] · **Type:** ✍️ Blogs · **Author:** Kirwin Hampshire · **Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[Unknown (country)\|Unknown]]

## Summary

A widely-shared personal essay by a pure-mathematics PhD student describing a 'spiritual crisis' over AI's advances in mathematics. It names the Leiden Declaration directly and judges it an insufficient response to what the author frames as a loss of meaning in mathematical discovery.

## Key quote

> The recent Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics is, to me, a well-muffled scream.
