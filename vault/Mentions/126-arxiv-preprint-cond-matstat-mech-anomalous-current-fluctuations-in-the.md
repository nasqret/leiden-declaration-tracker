## Summary

A statistical-mechanics preprint whose section 1.2, 'The use of AI in this work', states that the author's disclosure of AI use and assignment of human responsibility follow the recommendations of the Leiden Declaration, which is cited in the reference list. The paper reports predictions for long-time current distributions in a kinetically constrained hopping model and notes an accompanying paper containing an extensively AI-generated candidate proof.
