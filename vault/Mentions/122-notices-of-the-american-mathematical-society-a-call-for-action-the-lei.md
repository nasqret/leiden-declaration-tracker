## Summary

A feature in the Notices of the AMS (Volume 73, Number 8, September 2026, from p. 644; DOI 10.1090/noti3386) by Siobhan Roberts, executive editor of the Notices, telling the story of how the declaration came about — an idea first floated in a pub near Cambridge during the Isaac Newton Institute's “Big Proof” workshop by Ursula Martin, Michael Harris and Rodrigo Ochigame; Harris's four-point plan pitched to the 60 participants of the September 2025 Leiden “Mechanization and Mathematical Research” workshop; and eight months of drafting by 16 authors convened by Jim Portegies in consultation with more than 30 further mathematicians. It carries interviews with Portegies and Bryna Kra and endorsement statements from Fields Medallists Peter Scholze and Terence Tao, and then reproduces the FULL TEXT of the declaration — preamble, values, threats, recommendations and the complete author list — in the pages of the AMS's membership journal.
