# A Call for the Ethical Use of AI in Mathematics

> **Outlet:** [[UC San Diego Today]] · **Type:** 🏛️ Institutions & societies · **Author:** Michelle Franklin · **Date:** 2026-07-20 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United States (country)\|United States]]

**Link:** https://today.ucsd.edu/story/leiden-declaration

## Summary

UC San Diego's news office covers the Leiden Declaration through Karthik Ganapathy, a UCSD postdoctoral fellow and one of the declaration's 16 authors. The piece describes the declaration as a framework addressing AI's role in mathematics, and reports Ganapathy's account of how corporate involvement and underfunded academia threaten the discipline's autonomy over its own research direction.

## Key quote

> The direction of research is often the result of years of deep thought among subcommunities of mathematicians across generations, a process that remains the hallmark of basic research.
