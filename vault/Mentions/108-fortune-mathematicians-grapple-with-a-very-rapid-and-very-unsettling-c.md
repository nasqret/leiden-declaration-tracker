# Mathematicians grapple with a 'very rapid and very unsettling change' as AI cracks yet another century-old problem

> **Outlet:** [[Fortune]] · **Type:** 🧪 Science & tech magazines · **Author:** Eva Roytburg · **Date:** 2026-07-21 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United States (country)\|United States]]

**Link:** https://fortune.com/2026/07/21/ai-solves-jacobian-conjecture-levant-alpoge-claude-fable-5/

## Summary

Fortune reports on the counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture found with the help of Claude Fable 5 and announced by Harvard/Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge, and places the mathematical community's reaction in the context of the Leiden Declaration published the previous month. It quotes Akhil Mathew of the University of Chicago on the pace of change, particularly for junior mathematicians.

## Key quote

> In June, 16 researchers from 15 universities published the Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics, urging the profession to set guardrails around transparency, attribution, and peer review before AI reshapes what mathematical knowledge even means.
