# IMU Endorses Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics

> **Outlet:** [[American Mathematical Society (AMS)]] · **Type:** 🏛️ Institutions & societies · **Author:** American Mathematical Society · **Date:** 2026-06-30 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[United States (country)\|United States]]

**Link:** https://www.ams.org/news?news_id=7666

## Summary

An AMS newsroom item announcing that the American Mathematical Society, as the U.S. adhering organization to the International Mathematical Union, supports the Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics. It describes the declaration as a community-developed statement calling for coordinated action on the opportunities and challenges of AI in mathematical research, notes the IMU endorsement, and carries statements from AMS President Ravi Vakil and incoming president Joe Silverman.

## Key quote

> There is a great deal of poorly informed discussion in the media on this topic. Mathematicians are best positioned to speak with knowledge and experience, and it is important that our voices are heard. The authors of the declaration have based their recommendation on the fundamental values of our profession. — Ravi Vakil, President, American Mathematical Society
