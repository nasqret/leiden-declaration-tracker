# Leiden Declaration sparks global debate on the future of mathematics and AI

> **Outlet:** [[Universiteit Leiden (nieuws)]] · **Type:** 🏛️ Institutions & societies · **Author:** Leiden University · **Date:** 2026-06-25 · **Language:** English · **Country:** [[Netherlands (country)\|Netherlands]]

**Link:** https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/news/2026/06/leiden-declaration-sparks-global-debate-on-the-future-of-mathematics-and-ai

## Summary

A follow-up item from Leiden University reviewing the declaration's reception three weeks after publication: over 2,300 signatories at the time of writing, coverage in the New York Times, Scientific American, Nature and the Indian Express, and a spread of reactions ranging from support to arguments that AI should be embraced or that the declaration does not go far enough. It quotes Robbert Dijkgraaf on the scale of the transformation.

## Key quote

> Scientific research is in the midst of a major transformation through the impact of AI, and mathematics is one of the disciplines most fundamentally affected.
